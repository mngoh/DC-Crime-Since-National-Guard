# Records requests: where the Guard was posted

Drafts to file. They ask for the records that would replace the reconstructed post list with the real one. Confirm each address on the agency's own page before sending; these were collected October 8, 2026.

The key lead is DC v. Trump, No. 25-cv-3005 (D.D.C.), ECF 83-1, filed October 31, 2025. It contains a DC Guard daily update for September 12, 2025 (Bates DCNG_DEF_00000024-25), which refers to Guard support at "18x designated Metro Stations" without naming them. It also contains fragmentary orders (FRAGOs) that assign task forces to stations, with the station names redacted. Those orders point to "Attachment 1 to FRAGO 8" and an "Operations Overlay" appendix.

---

## 1. DC Army National Guard (federal FOIA)

**To:** FOIA Manager, District of Columbia Army National Guard, 2001 East Capitol St SE, Washington, DC 20003-1719. ng.dc.dcarng.mbx.foia@army.mil. Copy: National Guard Bureau FOIA, ngb.foia@army.mil.

> Under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request the following records of Joint Task Force-District of Columbia (JTF-DC) for the period August 11, 2025 to the date of search:
>
> 1. Attachment 1 to FRAGO 8, and the "Operations Overlay" appendix referenced in JTF-DC fragmentary orders, as they relate to the DC Safe and Beautiful mission.
> 2. All fragmentary orders (FRAGOs) and operation orders that assign units or task forces to Metro stations, patrol areas, or fixed posts within the District of Columbia.
> 3. The list of the "designated Metro Stations" referred to in DCNG daily updates (for example, the September 12, 2025 update produced in DC v. Trump, ECF 83-1, Bates DCNG_DEF_00000024), and any later versions of that list.
> 4. The daily DCNG operational updates or roll-ups that list patrol locations, as sent to Headquarters, Department of the Army.
> 5. Records showing the number of service members assigned to each location by day or week.
>
> I am not asking for personal information about service members. Names and personal identifiers may be redacted. Location and date information is the substance of this request.
>
> I am a researcher with Specta LLC. The results will be published, with the methods and data, in a public analysis of whether the deployment changed crime where troops were posted. I request a fee waiver under 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii), because disclosure is in the public interest and is not primarily in my commercial interest. If a waiver is denied, I agree to fees up to $50; please contact me before exceeding that.
>
> Electronic copies are preferred. If any part is withheld, please release the rest and cite the exemption for each withholding.
